## Main stories

### 1. QUAD SUMMIT IN MANILA
- The leaders of the Quad — US President Donald Trump, Indian PM Narendra
  Modi, Japanese PM Sanae Takaichi and Australian PM Anthony Albanese — met
  in Manila on Tuesday 17 Nov, the first in-person leaders' meeting of the
  grouping in two years and Trump's first in his current term.
- Five outcomes announced: a Quad Business Leaders' Forum (Mumbai, 2027),
  counterterrorism cooperation, Indo-Pacific maritime surveillance, cable
  connectivity, and cyber cooperation.
- Sources: The Japan Times, "Trump joins 'Quad' summit for first leaders'
  meeting in two years" (updated 18 Nov 2026 07:36 JST);
  The Economic Times, "Quad leaders unveil 5 outcomes at Manila summit"
  (17 Nov 2026).

### 2. UKRAINE HITS CRIMEAN BRIDGE; RUSSIA HITS KYIV BRIDGES
- Ukraine struck the 19-km Crimean Bridge (Kerch Strait) for a second
  straight day on Tuesday 17 Nov; video verified by Reuters showed the
  bridge's main road arch collapsed into the strait. Ukraine's SBU said the
  arch and road span were destroyed; the Russian-installed governor said
  two people were killed and traffic suspended.
- The same night Russia struck Kyiv's North and South bridges over the
  Dnipro and partly damaged the metro, confirmed by Moscow.
- Source: Reuters via Jerusalem Post / Channels TV / Kyiv Independent,
  17 Nov 2026.

### 3. US LOSES MEASLES-FREE STATUS AFTER 26 YEARS
- The Pan American Health Organization announced on Tuesday 17 Nov that the
  US (plus Mexico and Bolivia) had lost measles elimination status, held
  since 2000, after the same strain circulated for over 12 months.
- CDC had counted 5,412 confirmed US cases in 2026 as of 12 Nov — the
  worst outbreak in more than three decades.
- Sources: Al Jazeera, "US officially loses measles-free status after
  26 years" (17 Nov 2026); Channel News Asia (published 18 Nov 2026
  04:32 +08:00); CDC joint statement, 17 Nov 2026.

### 4. IRAN–US STRIKES ESCALATE AROUND THE GULF
- Iran's Revolutionary Guard (IRGC) said on Tuesday 17 Nov its drones
  targeted a US-operated radar system at Sheikh Isa Air Base in Bahrain —
  the third such attack on Bahrain in five days. Bahrain said its defences
  intercepted the drones; no casualties or damage reported.
- Hours later US fighter jets struck an IRGC missile-launch site on
  Qeshm Island, Iran, in the Strait of Hormuz, in reply.
- Sources: Times of Israel, "US strikes Iran's Qeshm Island after Tehran
  attacks American radar site in Bahrain" (17 Nov 2026); Al Jazeera,
  "Kuwait reports drone attacks as Iran, US trade attacks on Qeshm and
  Bahrain" (17 Nov 2026 23:38 UTC); Gulf News (17 Nov 2026).
